Topic: Cloud Infrastructures

Cloud infrastructures refer to the virtualized resources and services provided over the internet, allowing users to access computing power, storage, and applications without owning physical hardware. In more simpler terms, cloud infrastructures enable individuals and organizations to use remote servers hosted on the internet to store, manage, and process data, rather than relying on local servers or personal computers.
