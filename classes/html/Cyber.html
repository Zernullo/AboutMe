Summary

Cybersecurity is the pratice of protecting computers, networks, data from unauthorized access, attacks, or damage. It involves oreventing, detecing, and responding to threats like viruses, malware, phising, and hacking. The key conepts in cybersecurity include: (CIA)

Confidentiality: Ensuring that sensitive information is only accessible to authorized individuals or systems.
Integrity: Maintaining the accuracy and consistency of data, preventing unauthorized modifications or alterations.
Availability: Ensuring that information and systems are accessible and functional when needed by authorized users.

General Security Concepts

Security Control

Security controls are safeguards or countermeasures used to protect information systems, data, and resources. They reduce risk by preventing, detecting, or mitigating security threats and vulnerabilities.

Security Controls

  • Various Security Risks
    • Safeguards or countermeasures used to protect information systems, data, and resources. They reduce risk by preventing, detecting, or mitigating security threats and vulnerabilities.
  • Assets are also varied
    • Physical, Data, Computer Systems
  • Why do we need security controls
    • To protect assets from threats and vulnerabilities
    • Prevent security events, minimize the impact of incident, and limit the damage

Control Cateogories

  • Technical Controls
    • Controls implemented using systems
    • Operating system controls
    • Firewalls, anti-virus
  • Managerial Controls
    • Administrative controls associated with security design and implementaion
    • Secuirty policies, standard operating procedures
  • Operational Controls
    • Controls implemented by people
    • Security awareness training, incident response
  • Physical Controls
    • Controls that prevent physical access to systems
    • Locks, security guards, surveillance cameras
